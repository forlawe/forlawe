How this desk works — premium patient care

1. Doctor finishes consultation and ticks LAHSMA as destination (can tick 1, 2 or 3 destinations: Lab, Pharmacy, Billing, MEGALEX, LAHSMA, Emergency).
2. Patient appears here with name + policy number + priority ⭐ if elderly/pregnant/child/wheelchair — seen first.
3. You check their card on LAHSMA web app (separate system), then press Clearance issued.
4. If that was their last stop, visit closes and they can go home. If doctor also sent them to Lab/Pharmacy, those still show as pending elsewhere.
5. Journey time shown for every patient — based on real hospital speed.

Questions hospitals ask

Do we need to buy new TVs or devices?

No. Use your existing TV + HDMI from a laptop, or cast from Android. One TV at reception, one at executive lounge if you have Fast Track. That's it.

Is this an EMR? Will it store medical records?

No. HospitalSuite is for patient experience only — booking, queue, flow, feedback. We don't store diagnosis, vitals, or prescriptions. Your existing medical records stay where they are.

What is Fast Track and how does it make money?

Fast Track is a quiet, private lounge where patients who choose speed are seen first at every desk. You set the fee and building name. Patients consent before paying. Even 15-20 Fast Track patients a month often covers the platform cost.

Will patients need to download an app?

No app needed. Patients book or join queue from their phone browser, get a private tracker link by WhatsApp. Works on small Android phones and slow network.

How do WhatsApp and SMS work?

We send confirmations and updates by WhatsApp first, SMS as backup. Patients never miss their turn because one channel failed. You only pay for what you send.

Can we use it for multiple branches?

Yes. Starter is per hospital, Pro adds branches with separate queues, TVs, and reports. Same system, many locations.

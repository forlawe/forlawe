How to set up TV with zero budget — including Executive Fast Track TV

  1. Take any Android phone, open Chrome, go to /tv/MAIN for main waiting area, /tv/FASTTRACK for Executive Fast Track TV (gold only), or /tv/DENTAL for Dental TV
  2. Tap Enable Voice once — TV will shout every call in English then Yorùbá, Hausa, Igbo with today's Nigerian voice (2M2F recycled daily)
  3. Adjust 🔆 Brightness slider per TV — 10% dim for night, 100% bright day. Toggle 🌙 Night mode auto dims 19:00-07:00
  4. Connect phone to TV via HDMI cable or Cast / Miracast — TV now shows big live board. Executive TV shows only gold lane Fast Track
  5. Leave phone plugged to power — it auto-refreshes every 5 seconds, pings every 30s to stay awake

Waiting Area Main TV shows 8 stats, per-clinic counts, now serving, next 8, doctors ready, Lab/Pharmacy/Wards pending. Executive Fast Track TV shows ONLY Fast Track gold lane — for executive building lounge. Clinic TV shows only its clinic. All per-tenant. 4 languages inclusive.

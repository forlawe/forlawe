How a real Nigerian hospital visit usually works

Two tables, not one.

Stop 1 — Reception
You already have this. Patient booked or walked in. Staff tap Check-in. They get a number like E-014. Name never on the TV.
Stop 2 — Triage nurse
Nurse sees them first (2–4 minutes). Not to treat. To decide: can they wait? which room? one short note for the doctor.
Stop 3 — Consulting room
Each doctor has a room and only their list. Call next. Done. Or no-show.
After — you already have this
“How was your experience?” Low stars → recovery ticket. High stars → book again + share-link.

What each person would see

Patient (phone, no account)

  1. Gets E-014 at check-in (already works).
  2. “Please go to the Triage desk when your number is called.”
  3. After the nurse: “Please sit. You will be called for Room 2.”
  4. After the doctor: “How was your experience?” — the page you already have.

Triage nurse — one page, four large taps:

Routine Soon Urgent Emergency then send to Room 1 / Room 2 / Emergency bay.

Urgent and Emergency go ahead of Routine in that room. The skip is logged — not a secret favour.

Doctor — “My consulting room”: only their list, Call next, Done or No-show. No medical file in this version.

Safety rule you already set: the computer must never decide who is an emergency. A human nurse always taps Routine / Soon / Urgent / Emergency. We will not add diagnosis, drugs, or a full patient file here. This platform is the front door and the waiting chairs — not the doctor’s notebook.

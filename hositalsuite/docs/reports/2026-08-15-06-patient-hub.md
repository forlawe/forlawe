## Part 2 — The new patient page

### The problem I found

Your home page (`/`) **demanded a staff login**. A patient scanning your QR code at the
hospital gate landed on a login screen asking for a username and password. That was the
first thing every patient saw.

### What patients see now

Opening your website shows a clean menu of the six services, numbered and colour-coded:

| # | Service | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 📅 Book an Appointment | `/book` |
| 2 | 🎟️ Queue For A Service | `/queue/join` |
| 3 | 💬 Hospital Assistant | `/chat` |
| 4 | 📣 Complaint Of A Service | `/complaint` |
| 5 | ⭐ Feedback | `/feedback` |
| 6 | 💌 Share With A Friend | opens the phone's share menu |

Plus a red emergency notice ("go straight to A&E — do not wait online"), your hospital
phone numbers as tap-to-call, and links to check an existing complaint or booking.

### Things I made sure of

- **Staff are unaffected.** Signing in still takes you straight to your dashboard. `/` only
  shows the patient menu to people who are not logged in.
- **Works in all four languages.** English, Yorùbá, Hausa and Igbo — including the emergency
  notice. I checked each one renders correctly.
- **Built for a cheap phone.** Large tap targets, one item per row, and plain styling with no
  heavy frameworks, so it loads on older Android browsers over a weak signal.
- **Sharing works everywhere.** On a modern phone tile 6 opens the native share menu
  (WhatsApp, SMS, email in one tap). On older phones it falls back to a WhatsApp button and
  a "Copy link" button.
- **Your QR posters still track correctly.** If a poster tags a location (e.g. Reception),
  that tag is carried through to whichever service the patient picks, so your referral and
  location statistics stay accurate.
- **One share code, not thousands.** I verified the hospital-wide referral link is reused
  rather than created fresh on every page view.
- **Fails safely.** If the share link can't be built for any reason, the page still loads
  normally.

### Bonus fix

Your staff dashboard now has its own proper address at `/dashboard`. Previously it lived at
`/`, which is what forced the login-wall onto patients.

## 2. What I built today

### ✅ Requirement 3 — Department: HOD name + phone
Creating a department now **requires** the HOD's full name and phone number. They're stored on
the department itself, so you can record a head of department who doesn't have a login yet —
and the number survives even if you later change the linked staff account.

If you pick an existing staff member as HOD, their name and phone fill in automatically.
Phone numbers are cleaned up as you save: `0803 111 2222` becomes `08031112222`.

### ✅ Requirement 4 — User management
- **Fixed a bug that was breaking "Create user" completely.** The form was submitting to the
  wrong web address, so nothing happened when you pressed the button. It has been broken this
  whole time. It works now.
- **Department** can be assigned when creating a user, and changed when editing.
- **Approval**: accounts can now be marked as waiting for approval. They cannot sign in until
  you approve them, and they show with an amber "awaiting approval" tag with an Approve button.
  *(All your existing accounts were automatically marked approved — nobody got locked out.)*
- **Delete** a user permanently — but **only if they have no history**. If someone has signed
  an inspection or held duty, the system refuses and tells you to suspend them instead.
  That's deliberate: deleting them would leave their signed reports with no author, which
  would destroy the trustworthiness of your audit trail.
- **Safety guard**: you cannot delete or demote the last Super Admin, so you can never
  accidentally lock every administrator out.

### ✅ Requirement 5 — Bigger logo
| Where | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Top bar | 28px | **44px** |
| Patient pages | 56px | **88px** |

I also added a **resolution warning**: if you upload a logo smaller than 200 pixels, the system
now tells you the exact size and warns it may look blurry. Making the box bigger without this
would just have made a small logo look worse. **For a sharp result, upload at least 400×400
pixels — a PNG with a transparent background is best.**

### ✅ Requirement 6 — Admin Manager final comment
A **"📝 Your final comment"** box now appears at the bottom of the inspection form, with a
**🎤 Speak your comment** button for voice-to-text.

It appears on the review screen before you submit (so nothing is submitted unseen), on the
inspection detail page, and **on the PDF that goes to the MD/CEO** — I verified the text
actually appears in the generated PDF.

It also survives working offline: if you write a comment with no network, it's saved with the
queued inspection and sent when the connection returns.

**Already working, now protected by tests:** submit-once-per-day, and automatic draft saving.

## 3. Two other bugs I found and fixed along the way

1. **"Create user" was completely broken** (see above) — the form pointed at a web address that
   doesn't exist.
2. **A migration would have silently failed on your live database.** `user` is a reserved word
   in PostgreSQL, so the command to add the new columns needed quotes around it. Without the
   fix, the new user features would have appeared to work locally and quietly done nothing in
   production.

I also tidied up: phone-number validation was copy-pasted in four different files (now one
shared function), and I deleted a leftover template file that wasn't being used.

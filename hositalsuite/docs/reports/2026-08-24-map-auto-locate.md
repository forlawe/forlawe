# 1.7.5 — Map finds you when Location is on

**Date:** 24 August 2026  
**Version:** 1.7.5 (not pushed)  
**Voice reminders:** stay on.

## What you asked

The map should find the phone **by itself** as soon as Location is on. No extra tap.

## What happens now

| Where | What it does |
|---|---|
| I am here (staff) | Starts looking the moment the page opens. Hint says “Place found” when it has you. |
| I am here (Admin gate map) | Pin jumps to where you stand. Stretch the circle. Tap **Save**. |
| Admin → Sites | Same |
| Admin → Config | Same |

If Location is off, the page says **Turn on Location. The pin will drop by itself.** When you switch it on, it finds you without another tap.

You can still drag the pin if it is a few metres off. After you drag, it stops chasing the phone.

**Save is still a tap.** Finding you does not store the pin until you say so.

## How to try it on the phone

1. Stand at the gate.
2. Turn **Location** on.
3. Open **I am here**.
4. Allow the place question if the phone asks **once**.
5. The pin should land on you. Stretch the circle. Save.

Not live until you say push.

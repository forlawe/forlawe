Track Your Visit — Receive Your Queue Number

You'll get a number and a private tracker showing your live position through every stage of your visit (Reception, Billing, Triage, Doctor, Pharmacy) — first-time patients start at Reception, returning patients go straight to Records. Your number only shows on the TV screen — not your name.

1 · Which service do you need?

First-time patients start at Reception / Front Desk; if we already have your file, HIMS / Records takes you straight to it — no need to repeat paperwork. Fast-Track is premium at top — be seen faster in the quiet, private lounge.

2 · Your name
3 · Phone (optional, for updates)

Fast Track is our premium service. You are seen quickly in a quiet, private lounge. You pay a little more for comfort and speed. If you can afford it, choose Fast Track.

You don't need to pick Fast Track if your health condition need special attention. Just tell Reception/HIMS how we can help you, and we will prioritize your care.

After you join, you get a private tracker. It shows your position, estimated wait, and what to do next. Your number only shows on TV — not your name.

What happens next?
1. You get a number like A-012 and a private link
2. You go to the waiting area and watch the TV
3. When your number shows and you hear your name, go to the desk
4. Our staff will guide you through the rest

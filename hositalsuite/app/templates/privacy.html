We never ask for, and you should never enter, clinical details such as your diagnosis, test results or medications through these forms. The public display screens show ticket numbers only — never patient names.

3. Who can see it

Only authorised staff of {{ hospital.name if hospital else 'the hospital' }}: the Admin Manager on duty, the head of the department concerned, and hospital management. Access is controlled by role, and every view or change is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.

We use service providers to deliver SMS and WhatsApp messages on our behalf. They process only the phone number and message needed for delivery. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

About the assistant chat: this app answers most questions from the hospital's own prepared answers. When it can't, the message you typed — and the recent messages in that conversation — may be sent to an external AI service provider (currently Groq, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter) that helps generate a reply. They process that text only to produce the answer, under their own privacy terms. Please don't type clinical details (diagnoses, test results, medicines) into the chat or any form here — this app is not a clinical channel, and the person who can act on those details is a clinician you visit.

4. How long we keep it

Records are kept for the hospital's configured retention period (default: 6 years), after which personal identifiers — your name, phone number and the free-text description — are automatically erased. Anonymous statistics may be kept longer for quality reporting, because they can no longer identify you.

5. Your rights under the NDPA

  • Access — ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix anything that is wrong.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data, where the law allows.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting care you have already received.
  • Complain — to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you are unhappy with how we responded.

Make a data request →

6. How we protect it

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored using one-way scrypt hashing and are never readable by anyone, including us. Staff accounts use role-based permissions, failed logins are throttled and locked out, and all sensitive actions are written to a hash-chained audit trail that shows if anyone tampers with it. Backups are taken regularly.

7. Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top of this page.

📋 Bulk staff upload

Add many staff at once from a spreadsheet, instead of typing each one. Nothing is saved until you have seen a preview and pressed confirm.

Step 1 — Start from a template

Download one, fill in your staff, then upload it below. Already have a list in Excel? Skip this — we accept your own columns too.

⬇ Nominal roll (all staff) ⬇ Departmental list ⬇ Unit / section list

Step 2 — Upload your file

CSV or Excel (.xlsx), up to 2,000 staff per file.

Useful when you are uploading one department's list at a time.

What the file needs

A header row, then one staff member per line. Only Name is required.

ColumnRequiredNotes
NameYese.g. MRS ODEBE IDEHAI — titles are handled
DepartmentNoMust match a department you already have
PhoneNoe.g. 08028327098
EmailNo
DesignationNoe.g. HOD, Nurse, Admin Manager

We also recognise other common headings — Staff Name, Dept, GSM, Mobile, Cadre, Position and so on. Your existing spreadsheet will probably just work.

What happens to each new account
  • A username is created from the name (MRS ODEBE IDEHAI → odebe.idehai)
  • A random temporary password is set — you will see the list once, at the end
  • They must change that password the first time they sign in
  • They start awaiting your approval and cannot sign in until you approve them
